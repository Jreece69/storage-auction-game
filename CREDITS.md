# Credits

Every third-party asset, library and tool in this repo, with its source and
license. Commercial-use licenses only: CC0, CC-BY (credited here and in the
game), or paid licenses that say so. Each asset's license file goes in
`game/assets/third_party/<source>/`.

## In the game

| Name | Used for | Source | License | Credit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Godot Engine 4.7.2 | Game engine | https://godotengine.org | MIT, plus the third-party notices Godot bundles (fonts, FreeType and others) | Required: the license text must ship with the game |

Godot's MIT license requires its copyright notice to ship with the game. Phase
5 adds a licenses screen or file, following
https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/about/complying_with_licenses.html.
